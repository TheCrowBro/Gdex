Which roles need a licence

  • Audiophile: free, for personal useYour gigs on the calendar, the bands you like (scores, the times you saw them), venues with their house rules, notes and the tools. Free for good, with no licence and no free days to count. Not for work: what you do in music for pay, for clients or for a business needs a working role (see the terms).
  • Producer, Manager, Band, Solo artist, Scout and Jack of Trades: a licenceSessions, rehearsals, people, songs and setlists, backline, money and insurance, and the working boxes on a booking. Free for 30 days on each device, then with a licence.

A licence covers every role on the devices it is used on. When the free days are over without one, nothing is deleted: the working roles can still be opened, searched, printed and shared, and they can change things again once there is a licence. Or carry on free as an Audiophile, for personal use. Until licences are switched on at tourbook.crowmade.app, every role is free and the licence described on this page does not apply yet.

Buy a licence You can also buy from inside Tourbook, at any time during the free days or after them.

You are buying a licence to use Tourbook, not ownership of a copy of it. It can be cancelled in the cases the terms describe.

Already have a licence code?

  1. Once the desktop app is out, download it from this page and open it.
  2. Go to Settings → About & licence → Enter licence code.
  3. Paste the whole code from your email (it starts with TOURBOOK-) and press Activate. Tourbook needs the internet for this, once.

One licence works on up to 3 of your computers with the desktop app. To move it to a new one, use Release this computer on the old one, or choose which computer to move it from when you activate the new one. On the website, choose Sign in in Settings → About & licence and use the email address you bought with; Tourbook emails you a 6-digit code. Signing in on the website doesn’t use one of your 3 places.

A Vdex licence code doesn’t work in Tourbook: the two apps have licences of their own.

Installing, once it’s out

  • Windows: open Tourbook-Setup.exe. While Tourbook is new, Windows may show “Windows protected your PC” or say the app is not commonly downloaded: choose More info, check the publisher, then Run anyway. Tourbook updates itself after that.
  • Mac: open Tourbook.dmg and drag Tourbook into Applications. If macOS says it cannot check the app, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and choose Open Anyway.
  • Linux: make the file runnable (chmod +x Tourbook.AppImage, or tick “Allow executing file as program” in its properties) and open it. On Ubuntu 22.04 and later, install libfuse2 first (libfuse2t64 on 24.04).

Uninstalling keeps your work on the computer, so installing Tourbook again brings it back.

Bringing your work from the website

The website and the desktop app keep their own copies. To share one Tourbook between them, connect the same Google Drive in both (☁ at the top of Tourbook, then Connect Google Drive…): free, for every role, once Tourbook’s Google sign-in is switched on (until then Tourbook says Google Drive isn’t set up yet). Or, on the website, save a backup file (☁, then Save a backup) and bring it back in the desktop app.

Buying is covered by the terms of service (including refunds within 14 days), and the privacy policy explains what the licence check sends.
