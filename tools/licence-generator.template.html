First time only: make your keys

Do this once, before selling. It makes three keys, Gdex’s own (never use another app’s keys for Gdex):

  1. Your key (gdex-licence-key-1.json): makes free codes in this page.
  2. The shop key (gdex-licence-key-2-shop.json): Cloudflare uses it to make codes for paying customers. You paste it into Cloudflare once.
  3. A spare (gdex-licence-key-9-spare.json): replaces your key if it is ever lost or leaked. Keep it somewhere else, such as a USB stick in a drawer.

All three are locked with the passphrase below. Keep it in your password manager: without it the key files are useless, to you as well.
